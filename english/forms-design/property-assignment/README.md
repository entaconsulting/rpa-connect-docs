# Property Assignment

As we saw in the previous section, RPA Connect offers a wide variety of fields that we can use to collect data entered by users, as well as to display relevant information. All of them share certain basic properties such as their name and type, but there are many other features that will allow you to customize them to make the most of their functions. Below, we will review the most important properties and then delve into their application in different types of fields.

&#x20;By the end of this section, you will be able to:

* Locate and identify the most important properties to customize your form components.
* Understand their functionality and basic configuration parameters.
